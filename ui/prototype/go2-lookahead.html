What is real here

The 64-candidate library, the five score terms and their weights (1, 4, 2, 0.1, 0.1), the 0.65 risk fallback to stop_hold, and the latched stop all mirror go2wm.planning and go2wm.runtime.

What is a stand-in

A kinematic toy replaces LeWM. It reads simulator poses and guesses box mobility from appearance; the real planner sees only three 224 × 224 RGB frames. Surprise here is a scaled pose error, not latent RMSE. None of these numbers are results.

Goal cost toggle

With terminal only, the planner can deadlock near the goal: stop_then_creep ends closest, so it wins, but its executed first block never moves. The page flags this as a stall.
